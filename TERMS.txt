1. THESE TERMS AND THE LICENCE

These terms say how Lakay may be used. Who owns it, and who may use, copy or
share it, is set out in its licence (in About, and in Settings, Ownership).
If they ever differ, the licence applies.

2. WHO MAY USE LAKAY

Its owner, and a store and its people to whom the owner has given a
permission, as the licence says. Anyone may try the practice store on the
owner's own website, there and while the owner offers it (licence, clause
6.8).

3. THE STORE'S BOOKS ARE THE STORE'S

What a store records with Lakay, such as its books, receipts, reports and
notes, belongs to the store (licence, clause 3.6). The store is responsible
for what it records, and for having the right to record it. To keep its
records outside Lakay, a store can print its reports and save its sales,
receipts and stock as spreadsheet files (CSV).

4. FIGURES, REPORTS AND ESTIMATES

Lakay's reports, taxes, earnings and pay are worked out from what is
recorded. They are aids, not professional advice: not accounting, tax,
payroll, legal or financial advice. Pay is an estimate before taxes and
deductions. Check what matters with an accountant before you rely on it.
Lakay does not file taxes, run payroll or move money.

5. PAYMENTS, TRANSFERS AND BOOKINGS

Lakay records payments, transfers, top-ups and travel bookings; it does not
take payments or move money. Money moves through the store's own card
machine, its providers (such as RIA, CAM, Unitransfer or Boss Revolution)
and the websites where flights are booked, each under its own terms. Never
type a card number or a passport number into Lakay.

6. THE STORE'S PEOPLE AND ITS CUSTOMERS

The store's owner gives each person a password and their access. Lakay
records each person's sales, hours, shifts, pay and training, sealed. The
store tells its people and its customers what it records about them, and
keeps to the law where it is, such as the laws on work, privacy and tax
records. Lakay never asks for a customer's identity; a client's name and
phone on a booking are kept only if the store's owner turns that on.

7. FAIR USE

Do not use Lakay to break the law, to harm others, to keep records you have
no right to keep, or to get around the protection of anyone else's copy or
work.

8. PASSWORDS, THE MASTER KEY AND BACKUPS

Keep every password and the Master Key safe: they cannot be recovered for
you, and without them no one can open the books. Save a sealed backup every
day, and keep it away from the store's devices.

9. DEVICES IN STEP AND THE RELAYS

Only the store's owner adds a device to the store. The store's owner looks
after the store's own network, its router and its Mac minis, and removes a
device that is lost, stolen or given up (Settings, Devices in step).

10. SERVICES MADE BY OTHERS

The card machine's processor, the transfer providers, the websites where
flights are booked, and the private network app the store's owner chooses
(such as WireGuard or Tailscale) each come under their own terms. Using one,
you accept its terms.

11. NO WARRANTY AND NO LIABILITY

Lakay is provided as it is, as the licence says (clauses 11 and 12).

12. ENDING, CHANGES AND THE LAW

A permission ends as the licence says (clause 7). These terms may change
with a new release; Lakay then asks again. The law that governs them is in
the licence (clause 18), as settled for the United States and the United
