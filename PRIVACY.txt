1. WHO THIS NOTICE IS FROM

Lakay is made by Yannie D. Forest, its owner under its licence. Each store
that uses Lakay decides what it records, and is responsible for it.
Questions about this notice: the contact shown in Lakay, in About, Terms and
privacy.

2. WHAT LAKAY KEEPS, AND WHERE

The store's books stay on the devices the store uses Lakay on: its items,
prices, costs and stock; its sales, receipts and payments; its transfers,
top-ups and travel bookings; its people, their access, hours, shifts, pay
and training; its settings; and each person's sticky notes. In a browser, in
its own storage for Lakay's page; in the programs for Windows and the Mac,
in the person's own profile; on a phone or tablet, in the app's own storage.
Backups are files the store's owner saves where they choose. None of it is
sent to its maker.

3. SEALED WITH YOUR PASSWORDS

The books are sealed (AES-GCM, 256-bit) with one Store Key. The Store Key is
sealed again with a key made from the password of the store's owner, from
each person's own password (PBKDF2, 600,000 rounds), and from the Master
Key. Backups and the files that carry changes between devices are sealed
too. Without a password or the Master Key, no one can open the books, not
even Lakay's maker. Left readable, so that the door can work: the store's
name, the people's names and staff numbers, the Author Studio's choices, the
build number, and the technical facts needed to open the seal (such as its
salt).

4. WHAT LAKAY NEVER KEEPS

A card's number, a passport number, or a customer's identity. A sale records
what was sold and how it was paid; for a card, the card machine's approval
code and, if the store's owner asks, its last four digits. A transfer or
top-up records no customer's name, address or papers. A client's name and
phone on a travel booking are kept only if the store's owner turns that on;
never in the ledger, and erased when it is turned off.

5. WHEN LAKAY GOES ONLINE

Never by itself. Lakay's page is forbidden to connect anywhere, except to
the store's own relays once the store's owner has set them up: Lakay Relay,
on the store owner's own computers, such as two Mac minis. Lakay has no
analytics, no usage reports and no automatic update check. The camera, when
it scans a barcode, is read on the device only. When the web app is opened
from the owner's website, the website's host receives the request for the
page, under its own terms.

6. BETWEEN THE STORE'S OWN DEVICES

A device joins the store only with a joining file and a pairing code, shown
once on a device already in it. The joining file is a sealed backup: like
every backup, it shows the store's name, the people's names and staff
numbers, and the Author Studio's choices. Changes then travel sealed, by
file or through the store's own relays, which cannot read them; the relays
see only which device left changes, when, and how large they were.

7. YOUR CHOICES

Lakay's maker holds no data about you from Lakay, so it is in the store's
hands, on its devices. The store's owner can see the books, print and save
them (Reports), save a sealed backup (Settings, Backups), remove a device
(Settings, Devices in step), and erase the store from a device (Settings,
Erase). A person who wants to know what the store records about them asks
the store's owner. For data that another service holds, such as a card
processor or a transfer provider, ask that service.

8. CHILDREN

Lakay is made for running a store. It is not made for children under the age
the lawyer sets.

9. CHANGES

A new version of this notice comes with a new release of Lakay, with its
date; Lakay then asks again.
